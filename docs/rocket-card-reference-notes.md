# Rocket game card references

## Physical card overview photo

`docs/references/rocket-card-overview-20261113.png` is the user-provided overview photo for card-list reconciliation.

It visibly provides a reference for:

- The six role-card copies: Gym Leader, Gym Trainer, three Challengers, and Rocket Team member.
- The `くらえ！` and `よけて！` action-card faces.
- Pokémon-themed play cards, tools, items, and other effect-card designs.

Use this photo together with the official image ZIP and individual card images when matching card names, category, and artwork. It is a photographed overview, so do not infer exact printed text, quantities of play-card copies, or effects from the small text in this photo. Use the individual scans for those details.

## Card image ZIP

The user-provided `omaroke_cards_original.zip` is the primary source for the individual official card scans currently available to the project. Its manifest records the retrieved filenames and dimensions. The card overview photo is a separate reference and shows physical cards that should be matched to those scans before entering level-specific Pokémon data.
